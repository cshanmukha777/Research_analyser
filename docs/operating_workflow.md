# Research workflow and guardrails

## Before designing a candidate

- Read the existing candidate, failed-result, family, and experiment history.
- Confirm the objective and keep the requested baseline settings fixed.
- Select one economic mechanism and write the expected direction, horizon, publication timing, and kill criteria.
- Verify every field and operator in the BRAIN account for the exact instrument, region, delay, and universe. Store source evidence and verification date. The uploaded Genius screenshots do not verify field IDs or operator signatures.
- Check exact expression fingerprints and review near-duplicates by mechanism, data source, and horizon.

## Before manual simulation

- Queue only when every declared field and called operator is present in the scoped verified inventory.
- The queue policy is manual-only. Copy the expression into BRAIN and save its returned simulation ID and complete settings.
- Keep missing checks pending or unmeasured. Never infer a pass from an absent value.
- Do not record a result without an actual BRAIN result export or screenshot reference.

## After simulation

- Store all actual metrics, platform checks, warnings, and evidence references.
- Classify the bottleneck and append a learning and next step.
- Keep failed candidates in the same permanent registry.
- Record pairwise PnL correlation separately from BRAIN self-correlation, production correlation, and PowerPool correlation. Include period and observation count.
- Log submission state only after the account shows it. This repository does not submit.

## Research selection

Use research methods as references, not as proof of current performance. Treat outside repository metrics as historical self-reported observations. Distinguish original hypotheses from third-party alpha expressions and platform-derived field metadata. Do not copy expressions or protected platform data into this workspace without confirming applicable rights.
